## Rules the tests enforce
- Every word the child must READ (pick, spell, trace, reading lines) uses only letters and tones taught up to that lesson (no final consonants yet: those start at lesson 36).
- Reading lines have at most 6 words; every letter taught in a lesson is read at least once in it.
- Each hunt has 6 distinct pictures, 3-5 of them right.
- The review grids block exactly the spellings Vietnamese forbids (c/k, g/gh, ng/ngh before e ê i).
