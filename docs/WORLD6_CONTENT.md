# World 6 (crane truck): lessons 79-93 and the end-of-term review, book pages 142-169

Every lesson was read from the page images (three pages per picture) before the data was written.
What follows the book: the vần and their order, the picture words of each page (where an emoji exists), the story plots (from the
picture questions on pp. 144, 154 and 164). What is ours: all reading sentences (the book's texts are copyrighted; ours use only letters
and whole vần taught so far, checked by tests), story wording, pictures (Noto emoji).
