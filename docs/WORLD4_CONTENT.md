Every lesson was read from the page images (three pages per picture) before the data was written.
Tile order: 48 ôm ôp, 49 ơm ơp, 50 story, 51 review, **Ôn tập giữa học kì I** (book pp. 92-95), 52 um up, 53 uôm, 54 ươm ươp,
55 an at, 56 story, 57 review, 58 ăn ăt, 59 ân ât, 60 en et, 61 ên êt, 62 story. That is 16 tiles.
What follows the book: the vần and their order, the picture words of each page (where an emoji exists), the story plots (from the
picture questions on pp. 90, 104, 114). What is ours: all reading sentences (the book's texts are copyrighted; ours use only letters
and vần taught so far, checked by tests), story wording, pictures (Noto emoji).

## Rules the tests enforce
Same as World 3: hunts have 6 distinct pictures with 3-5 right (the answer is computed from the word), reading words only use taught
letters and whole vần, no vần is read before its lesson, every taught vần is read at least once, spell tokens are in the audio list.
Final ng, nh, ch, c stay for World 5.
